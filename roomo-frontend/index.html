Holy Pradakshina

14 km Girivalam Path

Circumambulate holy Mount Arunachala barefoot in a clockwise path. Visit the eight guardian Shiva Shrines (Ashta Lingams) aligned with cardinal cosmic directions.

Duration: 3.5 to 4.5 hours
Silent Sanctum

Sri Ramanasramam & Caves

Immerse in silent meditation at the Samadhi hall of Bhagavan Ramana. Trek the serene pathway up the hill to Virupaksha Cave and Skandashramam overlooking the town.

Timings: 5:00 AM – 9:00 PM
Agni Sthalam

Annamalaiyar Temple

A 25-acre Dravidian marvel celebrating the Fire Element. Enter beneath the soaring 217-foot Eastern Raja Gopuram and witness the grand thousand-pillar hall.
